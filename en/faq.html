Frequently asked questions about ABHITEK

Short answers to what people ask most. If your question is not here, send it through the form on the Chips & Pricing page.

ABHITEK/FAQUpdated 24 November 2026

About ABHITEK

What is ABHITEK?

ABHITEK is an app that keeps the records of safety equipment and office inventory. Each item gets one NFC chip that is locked to it. Tap a phone on the chip and the record of the item opens: its holder, inspections, maintenance and condition.

Who is ABHITEK for?

For safety and rope access teams that manage harnesses, helmets, carabiners and rope, and for General Affairs teams that manage office laptops, phones, printers, desks and chairs. One person with their own gear can use it too.

How can we try ABHITEK?

Open the demo at app.abhitek.co. It runs in the browser with sample data and needs no sign-up. When it fits, start with a Pilot Pack: chips and a reader for your first 50 tools.

Which languages does the app come in?

Six languages: Indonesian, English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Arabic. Each person picks their own language.

Does the app have to be downloaded from an app store?

No. The desk app and the phone app open in a browser. The phone app can be added to the home screen from that browser.

Pricing and quota

Is there a subscription fee?

No. The app is free up to 100 items per organisation and 75 items for a personal account. You pay for chips and readers, and for extra quota only when you have more items.

What happens when an extra quota pack ends?

Your data stays. The limit returns to the free quota, so only adding new items is held back until you buy a pack again. History, reports and export stay open.

What does it cost for a small office?

Up to 100 items the app is free. You buy only the chips, from Rp12,000 each, and one USB reader for Rp850,000 if you want to read chips at a desk.

Chips and phones

Can a chip be moved to another tool?

No. A chip is locked to one tool for good. A damaged chip is replaced with a new one, the reason is recorded, and the number of the old chip goes on the block list.

Which phones can read the chip?

An Android phone with NFC reads the chip straight from the Chrome browser. On an iPhone, the app opens the record of an item when you type its item code. At a desk, a USB reader reads the chip.

Can an item be recorded before its chip is fitted?

Yes. Register the item first with its internal code, then fit the chip at any time. Without a chip, the record of the item opens when you type its code.

Do we have to fit the chips ourselves?

You can, with the fitting guide in the Pilot Pack. Or an ABHITEK technician fits them at your location for Rp10,000 per item, with a minimum of Rp2,000,000 per visit.

Inspections

How often must safety equipment be inspected?

Follow the instructions of the maker and the safety rules of your workplace. Many makers ask for a check before each use and a periodic examination at least once a year by a competent person. ABHITEK keeps the due date of each tool and shows the tools that are due.

Can an inspection be done without a signal?

Yes. The inspection checklist in the phone app can be filled in without a signal. The result is kept on the phone and sent when the phone is connected again.

What happens when a tool fails its inspection?

The tool is held back. A damaged tool cannot be handed to anyone until its repair is done and recorded.

Your data

Who can see our data?

Only the people in your organisation, by their role. The ABHITEK platform admin sees account data and your chip orders, not your items, inspections or reports.

Can our data be taken out of ABHITEK?

Yes. The records of items, inspections and handovers can be exported to Excel at any time.
