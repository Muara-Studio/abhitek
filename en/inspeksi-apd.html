An inspection app for PPE and work-at-height equipment

ABHITEK records the inspection of safety equipment on the tool itself. Each harness, helmet, carabiner and rope gets one NFC chip. The inspector taps a phone on the chip, fills in the checklist, and the result stays on the record of that tool.

ABHITEK/PPE InspectionsUpdated 24 November 2026

The problems it solves

  • An inspection schedule in a spreadsheet is easy to miss, because nothing shows which tools are due today.
  • A number on a sticker can fade, come off or be swapped, so the history of one tool mixes with another.
  • A tool that was reported as damaged can still be taken to the field.
  • In an audit it is hard to show who inspected the tool, when, and with what result.

How it works

  1. Register the tool from the catalogue

    Pick the model from the 3,107 models in the catalogue. The brand, the model and the standards for that type of equipment fill in by themselves.

  2. Fit and lock the chip

    One chip for one tool. Once it is locked, the chip cannot be moved to another tool.

  3. Inspect from the phone

    Tap the phone on the chip, then fill in the checklist for that type of equipment. The checklist works without a signal too.

  4. Damaged tools are held back

    A tool that fails cannot be handed over until its repair is done and recorded.

What is recorded for each tool

Holder
Who holds the tool now. A handover counts only after the receiver confirms it.
Inspections
The date, the inspector, the answer to each point of the checklist and the final result.
Maintenance
The damage report, the repair that was done and the date it was finished.
Documents
Certificates and other documents of the tool, kept with its record.
Audit trail
Every change is recorded and kept at least 5 years.

Questions about inspections

How often must safety equipment be inspected?

Follow the instructions of the maker and the safety rules of your workplace. Many makers ask for a check before each use and a periodic examination at least once a year by a competent person. ABHITEK keeps the due date of each tool and shows the tools that are due.

Can an inspection be done without a signal?

Yes. The inspection checklist in the phone app can be filled in without a signal. The result is kept on the phone and sent when the phone is connected again.

What happens when a tool fails its inspection?

The tool is held back. A damaged tool cannot be handed to anyone until its repair is done and recorded.

Which phones can read the chip?

An Android phone with NFC reads the chip straight from the Chrome browser. On an iPhone, the app opens the record of an item when you type its item code. At a desk, a USB reader reads the chip.
