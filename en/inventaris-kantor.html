An office asset inventory app for General Affairs

ABHITEK records office items one by one. Each laptop, phone, monitor, printer, desk and chair has one NFC chip and one record: who holds it, which room it is in, and its condition.

ABHITEK/Office InventoryUpdated 24 November 2026

The problems it solves

  • An inventory list in a spreadsheet goes out of date fast, because moves are not recorded when they happen.
  • When an employee joins, moves or leaves, it is not clear which items they received and returned.
  • A broken item is found only when the next person needs it.

How it works

  1. Register the item from the catalogue

    The catalogue holds models of laptops, desktops, monitors, phones, tablets, printers, projectors, chairs, desks and cabinets from 12 makers.

  2. Record its location

    A location is written as a path, for example Main Warehouse, Storage Room, Rack 1. Items are easy to find at a stock count.

  3. Two-sided handover

    An item counts as held by an employee after that employee confirms, by tapping the chip or entering a code.

  4. Report damage from the phone

    Whoever holds the item can report damage. The report goes into the repair queue.

What kind of office it suits

  • An office that wants to know the number and condition of its items without a count from zero.
  • A General Affairs team that handles item handovers for many employees.
  • An organisation that also has safety equipment: both are recorded in one app.

Questions about office inventory

What does it cost for a small office?

Up to 100 items the app is free. You buy only the chips, from Rp12,000 each, and one USB reader for Rp850,000 if you want to read chips at a desk.

Can an item be recorded before its chip is fitted?

Yes. Register the item first with its internal code, then fit the chip at any time. Without a chip, the record of the item opens when you type its code.

Can our data be taken out of ABHITEK?

Yes. The records of items, inspections and handovers can be exported to Excel at any time.

Who can see our data?

Only the people in your organisation, by their role. The ABHITEK platform admin sees account data and your chip orders, not your items, inspections or reports.
