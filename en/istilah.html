Terms used in equipment records

Short meanings of the terms used in ABHITEK and in the records of safety equipment and office inventory.

ABHITEK/GlossaryUpdated 24 November 2026

Equipment and its checks

PPE (personal protective equipment)
Equipment a person wears for protection against hazards at work, for example a helmet, a harness and gloves.
Rope access
A way to work at height with ropes as the means to go up, to go down and to be held. Its equipment includes harnesses, descenders, ascenders, carabiners and rope.
Pre-use check
A short check by the user before the equipment is used.
Periodic inspection
A thorough examination at set intervals by a competent person, with a recorded result.
Inspection checklist
The list of points to check for one type of equipment. In ABHITEK the checklist follows the type of equipment.
Held back
The status of a tool that is damaged or failed its inspection. It cannot be handed over until its repair is recorded as done.

Records and identity

Equipment record
One sheet of history for one tool: purchase, registration, holders, inspections, maintenance, until the tool is disposed of.
NFC chip
A small chip without a battery that a phone or a reader reads from a few centimetres away.
UID
The unique number that the factory puts into an NFC chip. This number cannot be changed.
Internal code
The code your organisation gives to an item, for example ORG1-APD-0451. It is not the serial number of the maker.
Maker serial number
The number the maker prints on the equipment. It is kept in its own field, apart from the internal code.
Main catalogue
The list of models from makers that can be picked when an item is registered, so nobody types the specification again.

People and process

Handover
The move of an item from one holder to another. It is recorded after the receiver confirms.
Holder
The person who is responsible for an item at the moment.
Audit trail
The record of every change: who, when and what was changed. ABHITEK keeps it at least 5 years.
General Affairs
The office department that looks after items, rooms and work facilities.
Occupational safety and health
The safety and health of people at work. The safety team looks after safety equipment and its inspections.
Item quota
The number of active items an account can record. The free quota is 100 items per organisation; a quota pack adds to that number for 6, 12 or 24 months.
