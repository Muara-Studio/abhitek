Questions we hear often.

Is there a subscription fee?

No. The app is free up to 100 items per organisation and 75 items for a personal account. You pay for chips and readers, and for extra quota only when you have more items.

What happens when an extra quota pack ends?

Your data stays. The limit returns to the free quota, so only adding new items is held back until you buy a pack again. History, reports and export stay open.

Can a chip be moved to another tool?

No. A chip is locked to one tool for good. A damaged chip is replaced with a new one, the reason is recorded, and the number of the old chip goes on the block list.

Who can see our data?

Only the people in your organisation, by their role. The ABHITEK platform admin sees account data and your chip orders, not your items, inspections or reports.

Do we have to fit the chips ourselves?

You can, with the fitting guide in the Pilot Pack. Or an ABHITEK technician fits them at your location for Rp10,000 per item, with a minimum of Rp2,000,000 per visit.
