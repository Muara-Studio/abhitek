ABHITEK updates

Short notes on what changed in the app, the catalogue and the site. The newest is on top.

ABHITEK/UpdatesUpdated 24 November 2026

November 2026

  1. 24 November 2026
    New addresses

    The site moved to abhitek.co and the app demo to app.abhitek.co. Old links are forwarded to the new addresses.

  2. 24 November 2026
    The site in five languages

    The site is available in Indonesian, English, Korean, Japanese and Arabic, with guide pages and a questions page.

  3. 21 November 2026
    A shorter menu

    The site menu has three items: Tool Catalogue, The App, and Chips & Pricing. The chip page joined the pricing page.

  4. 20 November 2026
    Office equipment joins the catalogue

    The main catalogue gained 275 office models from 12 makers: laptops, monitors, phones, printers, chairs, desks and more.

  5. 20 November 2026
    A new look for the phone app

    The bottom buttons of the phone app were tidied: yellow marks actions only, and the buttons no longer cover the page.

  6. 19 November 2026
    Item list with location and photos

    In the desk app the location of an item shows as a path, and the item list can be seen as a photo gallery.
